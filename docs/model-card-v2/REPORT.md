# Model Card v2: final report

Branch `feature/model-card-v2`, 2026-11-20. Nothing has been pushed or merged, and no destructive database command has been run. Your `dev.db` was never written to; the sample run used a copy.

## 1. What changed

Model Card v2 turns the v1 card into an evidence-backed, risk-based governance record. The v1 card, its endpoints, its PDF and its data are unchanged; v2 is behind `GOV_E4_2_MODEL_CARD_V2` (on by default under `GOVERNANCE_V2`) and returns 404 when off.

**Slice 1, foundation**
- Field catalogue (spec §5) with requirement levels, applicability rules, provenance and the six field statuses (NOT_ASSESSED, UNKNOWN, POPULATED, NOT_APPLICABLE, EVIDENCE_PENDING, VERIFIED). NOT_APPLICABLE needs a written justification and the person who decided.
- AI and code-scan text is held as a suggestion beside the value and counts only after a named person accepts or edits it. Imported v1 values are visible but unaccepted.
- Record types (including MODEL_CONFIGURATION), record links, configuration resolution (default model and tenant overrides), consistency checks, owner checks (`users.accountType`, a service account cannot be an owner).
- v1 → v2 migration with a dry run, run once, additive, inside one transaction per organisation.

**Slice 2, governance engine**
- Framework registry: 20 seeded entries, every one `UNVERIFIED_REQUIRES_LEGAL_REVIEW`, holding identifiers, dates and our own one-line paraphrases only (no standard text). Revisions are append-only; assessments pin the revision.
- Control assessments with 8 statuses; evidence from PARTIALLY_IMPLEMENTED up; a different person verifies; effectiveness needs test evidence.
- Risk engine with hard rules 1–5, explanation of every input, configurable thresholds, acceptance from the existing risk register.
- Four separate scores (documentation, evidence, control effectiveness, regulatory), each with numerator, denominator and method. They are never combined.
- Finalisation gate, workflow DRAFT → RETIRED with separation of duties, approval with conditions, change-triggered reassessment, next review date.
- "Compliant" removed from user-facing text (Checkpoint 1, decision 1).

**Slice 3, outputs and UI**
- **Version diff**: `GET …/card-v2/versions/:a/diff/:b`, either side may be `live`. Lists field, control, score, summary and workflow changes; fields empty in both are skipped; acceptance alone counts as a change.
- **Export**: `GET …/card-v2/export?format=pdf|json&version=`. Generated from the same catalogue as the screen:
  - document control (status, workflow state, SHA-256 content hash, generated by and when, catalogue version)
  - applicable sections only, with requirement, status, provenance marks ("AI SUGGESTION, not accepted", "imported from v1", "awaiting acceptance"), N/A justifications, evidence references, verifier and framework refs
  - the four scores with their method, gate blockers, risk explanation, framework mapping with in-force dates
  - approval history up to that version, with conditions
  - the fixed disclaimer on page one and in every page footer
  - a **DRAFT watermark on every page** unless the version is APPROVED
  - sensitive values are never printed
  - every export writes a `CARD_EXPORTED` ledger event
- **Console card tab** (console-ui, `/dashboard/`, model page → Model card). It replaces the v1 tab when the v2 flag is on, and the v1 tab still shows when only v1 is on.
  - Summary: record type, the four states, internal risk tier (inherent and residual), regulatory classification shown separately, next review, the four scores with denominators and methods, the blockers list (each blocker scrolls to its field), warnings, migration note, disclaimer.
  - Sections: applicable only, with a "Show N/A sections" toggle. Per field: requirement badge, "why required" text, help tooltip, status, provenance badge, "not accepted" mark, AI or scan suggestion in a marked box with Accept, evidence, verifier, framework refs. Actions: enter or edit (typed editor per data type), accept, unknown, not applicable with justification, attach evidence, verify.
  - Framework mapping with the "not evidence of compliance" note, unverified badges, controls with status, in-force dates and an assessment form.
  - Workflow panel, approvals with conditions and superseded marks, risk engine explanation, versions with snapshot, PDF and JSON export per version, and the compare view.
  - Checked in a browser against the real migrated card at desktop and phone width.

- **Registry content is a seed, not legal advice.** Every entry stays unverified until the review below, and the registry can only be changed by a platform admin through a new revision with a reason.

## 6. Legal-review checklist

Each item needs a named reviewer. When an entry is confirmed, an admin records it as a new registry revision (`POST /api/v1/governance/framework-registry/:id/revisions`) and sets `verification_status` to `LEGAL_REVIEWED`.

**Registry entries and dates (all 20 are `UNVERIFIED_REQUIRES_LEGAL_REVIEW`)**
- [ ] **EU AI Act (`eu-ai-act-2024`)**: confirm entry into force 2024-08-01 and the phase dates seeded from the spec: prohibitions and AI literacy 2025-02-02, GPAI 2025-08-02, Article 50 transparency 2026-08-02, Annex III high-risk 2027-12-02, Annex I high-risk 2028-08-02. The last two reflect the proposed EU "Digital Omnibus" postponement; confirm whether it has been adopted and on what terms.
- [ ] **EU date discrepancy**: the existing EU AI Act pack (`eu-act.model.ts:96`) still uses the original high-risk date. It was left unchanged (decision 2). Decide which is right and align both.
- [ ] **EU control list and roles**: Articles 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 12, 14, 15, 27, 50, 53, 55, 72 and 73, and the provider and deployer role assigned to each.
- [ ] **GDPR**: in force 2018-05-25; confirm which articles the card should map.
- [ ] **India DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025**: commencement is phased and the date is seeded as unknown ("full obligations expected by May 2027"). Confirm each phase date.
- [ ] **India IT Rules 2026**: confirm the instrument name, date and the intermediary trigger (`R_IN_INTERMEDIARY`).
- [ ] **India MeitY AI governance guidelines 2025**: non-binding; confirm the title and publication date.
- [ ] **RBI FREE-AI** is **not** in the v2 registry (the v1 pack still covers it). Decide whether to add it.
- [ ] **Korea AI Basic Act**: seeded in force 2026-01-22; confirm the date and the high-impact definition used for `kr_high_impact`.
- [ ] **China AI content labelling measures and GenAI service filing**: dates unknown; confirm the instruments and dates.
- [ ] **Colorado (`us-co-sb26-189`)**: confirm the bill number, effective date and the "consequential decision" trigger (`R_US_CO_CONSEQUENTIAL`). The original Colorado AI Act date has moved more than once.
- [ ] **US federal placeholder**: decide which federal guidance, if any, to add.
- [ ] **NIST AI RMF 1.0 (2023-01-26) and NIST AI 600-1 (2024-07-26)**: confirm the dates and the twelve GenAI risk areas.
- [ ] **ISO/IEC 42001:2023, 23894:2023, 42005:2025, 22989:2022**: confirm editions and dates (42005 and 22989 are seeded as to be confirmed).
- [ ] **OECD AI Principles, Singapore Model AI Governance Framework and its GenAI version**: confirm editions and dates.

**Wording and copyright**
- [ ] **ISO paraphrases**: confirm that the stored clause numbers and our one-line paraphrases (for example "Annex A group: policies related to AI") do not reproduce standard text.
- [ ] **ISO 42001 framing**: the card says it is an organisational standard and the card "cannot show conformity". Confirm the wording.
- [ ] **Fixed disclaimer**: "This model card documents the system. Framework mappings are not evidence of compliance or certification." Confirm it, and whether it should also appear in the JSON export (today it is in `card.disclaimer`).
- [ ] **"Obligation" vs "control" vs "practice"**: "obligation" is used only for law and regulation. Confirm.

**Risk method (for the risk owner as well as legal)**
- [ ] **Thresholds**: MEDIUM from 6, HIGH from 11, CRITICAL from 16, out of 23. These are my starting values; they are configurable per organisation.
- [ ] **Floors**: unknown or severe harm gives at least HIGH; major harm gives at least MEDIUM; a configuration that reaches every tenant, or whose reach is unknown, gives at least MEDIUM.
- [ ] **Risk approver roles**: default CISO or OWNER.
- [ ] **Evidence freshness**: default 365 days, test reports 180, monitoring records 90.
- [ ] **Review cycle**: default 12 months; confirm, or set per organisation.
