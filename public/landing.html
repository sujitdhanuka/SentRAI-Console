Responsible AI, operationalized

The 7 Sutras — as running software, not a poster on the wall

The RBI anchored FREE-AI in seven guiding principles. Here is how SentrAI turns each one into something your team does every day, with evidence to show for it.

1

Trust is the Foundation

"Trust is non-negotiable and should remain uncompromised."
In SentrAI: every model action — registration, edits, compliance runs, decommissioning — lands in an append-only audit ledger you can hand to a supervisor.
2

People First

"AI should augment human decision-making but defer to human judgment."
In SentrAI: when drift crosses the alert threshold, the circuit breaker trips and the model is flagged for mandatory human review before it keeps deciding.
3

Innovation over Restraint

"Foster responsible innovation with purpose."
In SentrAI: scanning a repo and registering a model takes minutes, not committee cycles — governance that keeps pace with shipping instead of blocking it.
4

Fairness and Equity

"AI outcomes should be fair and non-discriminatory."
In SentrAI: compliance validators check for documented fairness testing and bias controls on every model, and score the gaps you still owe.
5

Accountability

"Accountability rests with the entities deploying AI."
In SentrAI: no model exists without a named owner, and incidents carry SLA clocks — responsibility is assigned, timed, and tracked to closure.
6

Understandable by Design

"Ensure explainability for trust."
In SentrAI: each inventory entry records the model's purpose, data, and explainability evidence, so anyone can answer "what does this model do and why?"
7

Safety, Resilience & Sustainability

"AI systems should be secure, resilient and energy efficient."
In SentrAI: continuous drift monitoring, a BCP circuit breaker, DPDP-compliant PII masking, and Prometheus metrics keep models observable and fail-safe.

Code-level discovery

Regex plus tree-sitter AST analysis finds model definitions in real source, not self-reported forms. Scans local paths, GitHub, GitLab, and k8s/Triton serving infra.

Four frameworks, one score

Pluggable validators for the RBI's 7 Sutras, NIST AI RMF, the EU AI Act, and ISO 42001 — run them all against a model in one pass.

Statistical drift detection

Population Stability Index and 1-D Wasserstein distance computed on every reference/current window, with configurable warning and alert thresholds.

PII masking aligned with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act is applied across the console, so governance itself doesn't become a data leak.

🧾

Regulator-ready exports

One-click PDF compliance reports per model, immutable audit trails, and incident histories with SLA status — evidence in the format audits actually want.

🔌

Fits your stack
