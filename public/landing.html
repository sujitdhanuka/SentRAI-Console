Why governance stalls

A policy on paper doesn't stop a model from shipping.

As AI spreads across teams, repos and serving clusters, three gaps open between what your policy says and what's actually running in production.

Gap 01 · Visibility

You can't govern what you haven't found.

Models arrive in code, notebooks and serving infrastructure long before anyone fills in a register. The spreadsheet inventory is stale the day it's written.

How SentRAI closes it

Scans local code, GitHub and Kubernetes/Triton to build the inventory from what's really deployed.

Gap 02 · Enforcement

A rule nobody checks is a suggestion.

Sign-offs live in email threads, and a high-risk model can reach production with its screening, bias tests or oversight plan still missing.

How SentRAI closes it

Deployment gates and a CI step refuse promotion until the required evidence is in place, and list exactly what's missing.

Gap 03 · Evidence

Every audit turns into archaeology.

When the board or a regulator asks, teams rebuild the story by hand from scattered documents, and the numbers rarely match.

How SentRAI closes it

Model cards, board reports and Annex IV exports come from live data, backed by an append-only audit trail.

FAQ

Questions teams ask first.

Can't find yours? The coverage disclosure lists every requirement we handle and every one we don't yet.

Where does SentRAI run, and where does our data go?

SentRAI is self-hosted. It runs in your own environment on SQLite for evaluation or MariaDB/MySQL in production, so model metadata and evidence stay in your infrastructure and your jurisdiction.

How does it find models without a questionnaire?

It scans source code with regex and syntax-tree analysis (Python, JavaScript, TypeScript and Go), from an uploaded zip, a folder or a GitHub repository, plus Kubernetes and Triton serving configuration. You review the discovered components and choose which to govern.

Which frameworks are included?

NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001 always apply. You add regional packs for the EU AI Act, the US AI policy pack, Australia's DTA policy v2.1 and India's RBI FREE-AI, and SentRAI only asks the questions those packs require.

Can it actually stop a deployment?

Yes. Promotion to a live stage runs the deploy gate, which can warn or block depending on your settings, and lists exactly what's missing. The same check runs as a step in your CI pipeline.

What's in a model card, and how is it exported?

Model details, intended use, risk and impact, data and provenance, evaluation, governance and monitoring, each mapped to the frameworks in force. Cards are versioned as draft or final, signed off, and exported as PDF, Markdown or JSON.

Does using SentRAI make us compliant?

No tool can do that on its own. SentRAI helps you operationalise these frameworks and keep the evidence. Coverage is 257 of 275 applicable requirements, and the disclosure shows the remaining gaps.
